-------------------
SLA (Service Level Agreement) is a contract between a service provider and a customer that defines the 
level of service expected. It typically includes:

-Uptime guarantee (e.g., 99.9% availability)
-Response time for issues and support requests
-Performance benchmarks
-Penalties or compensations if the provider fails to meet the agreed terms

SLAs are common in cloud computing, hosting services, and IT support contracts.
